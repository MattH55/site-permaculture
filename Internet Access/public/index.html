Alberta CARI+ emergency access and measured broadband

This map covers all 6,203 Alberta dissemination areas from the 2021 Census. Each DA has a 24-hour emergency-access value: CARI+ scores (figshare 30721058; McGaughey and Peters, Canadian Geographies 2026) plus road-network minutes and kilometres to the nearest hospital-based 24-hour ED. Those scores are joined to M-Lab NDT measured download and upload speeds and ISED National Broadband Data 50/10 household-share bands. Use the map to look up emergency access and to compare rural internet performance.

Read about the map and the methods and data sources.

Uses CARI+ for 24-Hour ED Accessibility (standardized 0–18, normalized 0–1, March 2023). Click the map, search, or use your location to see your dissemination area’s scores and the computed road distance/time to the nearest 24-hour ED.
