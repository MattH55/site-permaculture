What You Get

Draw a rectangle or freeform shape around your property. The app returns real Alberta data — not generic estimates.

Your Parcel Location

Find your land by address, place name, or coordinates. See it snapped to the Alberta Township System quarter-section.

Slope & Elevation

Mean elevation, relief, slope percentage, and a 3D terrain view showing pond sites, catchment areas, and swale positions derived from HRDEM elevation data.

Water & Wells

Predicted well depth based on nearby pump tests, static water level, aquifer completion, well yield from regional data, and proximity to surface water.

Solar & Wind

NRCan solar incidence data for your municipality with monthly profiles. NASA wind rose showing primary and secondary wind directions with shelterbelt orientation recommendations.

Climate & Hardiness

Hardiness zone (NRCAN), frost-free days, temperature extremes, annual heat units (GDD), and a full monthly temperature profile from ERA5 reanalysis data.

Flood & Regulatory

FHIP flood hazard mapping status, regulated wetland flags, zoning links to county/municipality portals, and Water Act compliance notes for earthworks.

Wildlife & Biodiversity

iNaturalist observations near your parcel, deer pressure assessment, regional species richness screens, and nearby species that could fit your area.

Planting Recommendations

Species ranked by site fit with layered polyculture design. Includes food forest builds, shelterbelt design, and water features tailored to your zone.
