What Your Report Covers

Every report analyzes dozens of data points so you can make confident decisions about your land.

Water Availability

Find your property on the map and see its location relative to surface water, predicted well depths, and regional water sources.

Terrain & Elevation

Understand your land's elevation, slope, and relief — identifying ideal spots for ponds, catchment areas, and water management features.

Groundwater & Well Depth

Get predicted well depths based on nearby pump tests, static water levels, aquifer data, and expected well yield from regional records.

Solar Potential

See solar energy data for your area with monthly profiles, helping you plan solar installations and understand energy viability.

Climate & Growing Zone

Know your hardiness zone, frost-free days, temperature patterns, and growing degree units — essential for planning what to grow.

Flood Risk & Wetlands

Check flood hazard mapping status, regulated wetland flags, and Water Act compliance notes for earthworks on your property.

Tree Cover & Vegetation

Analyze satellite imagery to understand existing vegetation, tree cover density, and land health indicators.

Wildlife & Biodiversity

Discover species observed near your property, assess deer pressure, and understand the local ecosystem you'll be working with.

Development Opportunities

Get tailored planting recommendations, food forest designs, shelterbelt layouts, and water feature placements for your specific property.
