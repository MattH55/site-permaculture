Permaculture Built for Chinooks, Wind & Semi-Arid Stress

Calgary-region land is spectacular — and demanding. Rapid thaws, winter wind, variable snowpack, and summer dry spells punish weak design. Expanding Edge brings full design-build permaculture to southern Alberta properties: placement that respects aspect and exposure, water harvesting that earns its keep in dry years, and plantings selected for foothills hardiness — not catalogue fantasy.

Communities We Serve Around Calgary

Rocky View & North

Rocky View County, Airdrie, Crossfield, Balzac corridor — large acreages and estate properties ready for master planning and earthworks.

West Foothills

Cochrane, Bragg Creek fringe, Jumping Pound, mountain-adjacent holdings — wind, slope, and microclimate design are essential.

South Corridor

Okotoks, High River, Foothills County, Priddis, Millarville — lifestyle acreages and small farms integrating food, privacy, and water security.

East & City Fringe

Chestermere, Langdon, and eastern Rocky View — open exposure sites that benefit from shelterbelts and efficient water systems.

Services for Calgary-Area Properties

  • Site design & mapping — GIS-informed placement for buildings, water, plantings, and access
  • Shelterbelts & microclimates — Wind protection and chinook-aware species selection
  • Water harvesting — Swales, ponds, cisterns, and irrigation efficiency for drier southern sites
  • Food forests & orchards — Cold- and wind-tolerant guilds with realistic establishment plans
  • Implementation — Earthworks and planting support so designs become living systems

Site Conditions We Design For

Chinooks & freeze-thaw

Species and infrastructure choices account for rapid temperature swings that stress plants and soil structure.

Wind & aspect

Western exposure and ridge sites need shelter first. We design edges that create usable growing space.

Water security

Capture snowmelt and summer storms; store in soil and ponds; reduce reliance on costly irrigation alone.
