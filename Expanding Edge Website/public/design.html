What Data We Use

Topography & Elevation

Precise elevation data for slope, aspect, and water flow patterns on your property.

Climate Data

Temperature, precipitation, frost dates, and growing season information tuned for Alberta and the Prairies.

Soil Information

Soils atlas data showing types, classification, and management considerations for Western Canada.

Water Features

Watershed context, wet areas, and flow patterns to guide harvesting and drainage design.

Land Use Patterns

Current vegetation, structures, and adjacent use so recommendations fit real context.

Satellite Imagery

Current aerial view to map structures, vegetation, and existing features accurately.

Site Design Tool FAQ

Is this tool free to use?

Yes. Create designs, explore scenarios, and print your report at no cost — built for landowners across Alberta and Western Canada.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. Draw on the map and follow the prompts. No GIS background required.

How accurate are the recommendations?

We use government geospatial data and established permaculture principles. Professional on-site assessment adds further precision for earthworks and planting.

Can I save my designs?

Yes. Work saves locally in your browser so you can return, revise, download, or print.

Does it work outside Alberta?

Yes — use it for properties across Western Canada and beyond. On-site implementation is prioritized for Alberta and the West.

How do I implement the design?

Prioritize what matters most in your report, then contact us for consulting or full installation support.
