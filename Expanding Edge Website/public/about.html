Who we are

About Expanding Edge Permaculture

Building Canada’s largest permaculture implementation company — design that gets dug, planted, and proven on Alberta and Western Canadian land.

Our Mission

Expanding Edge Permaculture exists to professionalize regenerative land development in Canada. We help landowners design and implement systems that improve soil health, catch and store water, produce food and fibre, and build long-term ecological and economic value.

Too many permaculture projects stop at the whiteboard. Our standard is different: satellite-informed design, cold-climate expertise, and implementation capacity that scales — so Alberta acreages, Prairie farms, and Western Canadian stewards get living systems, not just PDFs.

The Ambition: Canada’s Implementation Leader

We are deliberately building the largest full-service permaculture implementation company in Canada. That means investing in tools, field methods, and regional knowledge so more properties can move from idea to earthworks without losing the integrity of good design.

Leadership for us is measured in hectares improved, water retained, food systems established, and clients who still harvest and regenerate five and twenty years later — starting in Alberta, expanding across the West, and setting a national bar for professional regenerative practice.

What We Do

Professional permaculture services for farms, acreages, homesteads, and land projects across Alberta and Western Canada:

  • Site Design Reports: Interactive mapping and analysis with actionable placement recommendations
  • Implementation & Earthworks: Swales, ponds, access, and infrastructure installed to plan
  • Food Forests & Planting: Cold-hardy guilds, shelterbelts, and polycultures for prairie and parkland
  • Water Systems: Harvesting, storage, and efficient distribution for variable Western climates
  • Homestead & Farm Planning: Zones, sectors, buildings, livestock, and multi-yield layouts
  • Consulting: Strategy, phasing, and regenerative management for private and institutional land

Our Approach

Permaculture design is built on three core ethics: Earth Care, People Care, and Fair Share. These guide every engagement — from a free design-tool session to a multi-year build.

We combine:

  • Ecological Science: Soil, hydrology, climate, and biodiversity principles
  • Proven Permaculture Practice: Decades of tested design methods, adapted to Canada
  • Local Knowledge: Alberta and Prairie climate, soils, species, and markets
  • Technology: GIS analysis, elevation models, and interactive mapping tools
  • Field Capacity: Implementation partners and methods that finish what design starts

Alberta & Western Canada Adaptation

Regenerative design here is not a copy-paste of coastal or tropical systems. Our consulting and builds account for:

  • Semi-arid south through parkland and boreal transition — with highly variable precipitation
  • Short growing seasons, extreme cold, chinooks, and wind exposure
  • Diverse soils: clay, loam, sand, and degraded cropland needing regeneration
  • Watershed stewardship, wet areas, and drought-period water security
  • Local markets for food, forage, value-added products, and ecological services
