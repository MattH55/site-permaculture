The Expanding Edge method

From Satellite Map to Living System

A repeatable design-build pipeline built to become Canada’s standard for professional permaculture implementation.

1

Map Your Land

Draw your parcel on our interactive tool or share survey boundaries. We pull elevation, slope, soils, climate, and watershed context for your Alberta or Western Canadian site.

2

Design the System

Receive placement recommendations for water, buildings, plantings, livestock, and access — prioritized by yield, resilience, and budget so you can phase with confidence.

3

Implement On the Ground

Optional: our crews and partners install earthworks, plant food forests, and set up infrastructure. Design without follow-through is not our model.

4

Steward & Scale

Monitor, adjust, and expand. We help you grow productivity and ecological function over decades — the long game of regenerative land stewardship.

Why Permaculture Design Matters in Western Canada

🌍 Regenerative

Build systems that improve soil organic matter, water retention, and biodiversity — critical on Prairie and parkland soils under climate stress.

💰 Economical

Lower long-term input costs through smart placement, gravity water, perennial production, and multi-yield landscapes.

🛡️ Resilient

Design for drought, late frost, wind, and market swings with diversified, self-reinforcing systems.

🌱 Productive

Stack yields — food, fuel, fibre, forage, habitat, and income — from the same acreage without mining the land.

Common questions

Permaculture in Alberta & Western Canada — FAQ

Do you only design, or do you implement too?

Both. Expanding Edge is a design-build company. We produce site designs and can install earthworks, plantings, and water systems so your plan becomes a living landscape.

Where in Canada do you work?

Alberta is our core territory. We also take projects in BC, Saskatchewan, and Manitoba, with remote design available across Canada. See our service areas.

Does permaculture work in Alberta’s climate?

Yes — when designed for it. We specialize in cold-climate placement, hardy species, shelterbelts, microclimates, and water systems that fit prairie and parkland realities.

What property sizes do you handle?

Urban lots, hobby acreages, full homesteads, and commercial-scale farms. The design tool and implementation model scale with your land and goals.

How much does a project cost?

Depends on size, earthworks, and planting scope. Start free with the design tool, then request a consultation for a phased budget tailored to your Alberta or Western Canada property.

How do I get started?
